ShipLink GÇö Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

ShipLink ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and delivery addresses you provide when creating an account.
  • Location Data: Real-time GPS location is collected for drivers to enable order tracking and delivery routing. Users who place orders may share their delivery address.
  • Payment Information: We use Paymob for payment processing. We store a tokenized reference and the last four digits of your card. We do not store full card numbers, CVV, or expiry dates.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for push notifications and analytics.
  • Order Data: Order history, items ordered, prices, and delivery status.
  • Communications: Chat messages between users and drivers within the App.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the App's services (order placement, delivery tracking, payments).
  • To enable real-time location tracking for delivery drivers.
  • To process payments securely through Paymob.
  • To send push notifications about order status and updates.
  • To communicate with you regarding your orders or account.
  • To improve and optimize the App.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Supabase: Our backend infrastructure provider (database, authentication, and real-time services).
  • Paymob: Payment processing (card data is tokenized; we never see full card details).
  • Google Maps / External Navigation: When a driver initiates navigation, the delivery address is shared with the selected navigation app (Google Maps, Waze, or Apple Maps).
  • Firebase (Google): For analytics and push notification delivery (FCM).
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Location Data

Drivers: Your real-time GPS location is shared with customers who have an active order, so they can track delivery progress. Location data is only shared during an active delivery and is not stored after the order is completed.

Location data is used for:

  • Real-time delivery tracking by the customer.
  • Optimizing delivery routes.
  • Improving service quality and delivery estimates.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Account data: Retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time.
  • Order history: Retained for 2 years for record-keeping and dispute resolution.
  • Location data: Not stored after the delivery is completed.
  • Payment tokens: Retained as long as you keep a saved payment method.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Opt out of push notifications via your device settings.
  • Revoke location access via your device settings.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL).
  • Row-level security (RLS) policies on all database tables.
  • Tokenized payment processing (card data never stored on our servers).
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App and updating the "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
